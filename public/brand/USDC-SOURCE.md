# USDC token logo

`usdc.svg` is the unmodified `Token Logo/USDC Token.svg` from Circle's official brand kit, downloaded September 16, 2026.

- Brand kit: https://www.circle.com/pressroom#brand-kit
- Asset pack: https://6778953.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/6778953/Pressroom/brandkit/logo-downloads/usdc.zip

Used to identify USDC in the token selection, amount, review, claim and landing interfaces. The artwork is owned by Circle; Luma's own brand remains separate.
